1. Summary

  • Direct Message does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
  • Phone numbers you type, message drafts, quick-message templates, and recent numbers and messages are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • We use analytics (Google Firebase Analytics and PostHog) to understand how the App is used and to improve it. This uses a random identifier created for your installation, not your identity.
  • Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see or store your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active on your installation.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone (for example, in the App’s private storage or in a shared container used by the Direct Message keyboard) so that the App works and remembers your preferences:

  • Phone numbers you enter or paste, and your recent numbers;
  • Messages you write and your recent messages;
  • Quick-message templates you create;
  • Settings such as language, selected country code, preferred messaging app, and “fast mode”.

We cannot see this data. It stays on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. If your device or operating system backs up app data (for example iCloud or Google device backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

When you tap to start a chat or send an SMS, the App passes the phone number and message to the messaging app or SMS app you chose (for example WhatsApp). From that point, your conversation is handled by that app under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Information we collect

We (and our service providers acting for us) collect:

  • Identifiers. A random installation identifier created by the App the first time it runs, plus app-instance identifiers created by Firebase and PostHog and an anonymous app user ID created by RevenueCat. These identify an installation, not you as a person, and they reset if you uninstall and reinstall the App.
  • Usage information. Which screens you open and which features you use — for example tapping “Send”, the number of times you have pressed Send, the number of templates saved, whether you turned on fast mode, whether you enabled the keyboard, how you answered the “Enjoying the app?” prompt, and when you viewed or interacted with subscription offers.
  • Session replays. PostHog may record how you move through the App’s screens (taps, scrolling, screen layout). All text and images are masked before recording, so replays do not show phone numbers, message text, or contact names.
  • Device and app information. Device model, operating system and version, app version, language and region settings, approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level), and diagnostic information such as App errors.
  • Purchase information. See section 4.
  • Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.

We do not collect the content of your WhatsApp, SMS, or other conversations, your contact list, your precise location, or what you type with other keyboards. We have designed our analytics so that phone numbers, message bodies, and contact names are never attached to analytics events or profiles.

This website is a static site. We do not use cookies or analytics on it. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Direct Message may offer premium features through auto-renewing monthly and yearly subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase, as described in our Terms and Conditions.

  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. When you buy on iOS, Apple processes the payment through your Apple Account. When you buy on Android, Google processes it through your Google Play account. Apple and Google collect your payment method, billing address, and similar details under their own privacy policies. We never receive or store your full card number or bank details.
  • What we receive. Through Apple, Google, and RevenueCat we receive transaction information needed to give you access to what you bought: the product purchased (for example monthly, yearly, or lifetime), price and currency, store country, purchase and expiry dates, renewal, cancellation, billing-issue, refund, and trial or offer status, and store transaction identifiers and receipts. This information is linked to the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID for your installation, not to your name or email.
  • Why we use it. To unlock and verify premium features, restore purchases on a new or reinstalled device, prevent fraud and abuse, handle refunds and support requests, meet tax and accounting obligations, and understand which plans are used (in aggregate).
  • Restoring purchases. When you tap “Restore Purchases”, the App asks Apple or Google which purchases belong to your store account and sends the result to RevenueCat to reactivate your access.

5. Device permissions

  • Contacts (optional). If you choose to save a number from your recents to your contacts, the App asks for permission to access your contacts so it can create or update that contact on your device. Contact data is processed only on your device and is never uploaded to us or to our analytics providers. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the App keeps working.
  • Clipboard. To offer number auto-detection, the App may read the clipboard when you use that feature to look for a phone number. Your operating system may show a notice when this happens. Clipboard contents are processed on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Internet. Used for analytics, purchase verification, and opening links.

6. Direct Message keyboard

The optional Direct Message keyboard lets you insert your saved templates in other apps. It reads your templates from storage shared with the App on your device.

  • The keyboard does not record, store, or transmit what you type, and it does not send your templates anywhere.
  • On iOS the keyboard does not request “Full Access”, so iOS does not allow it to use the network.
  • Your operating system may show a general warning when you enable any third-party keyboard. The only keyboard-related information we collect is whether the keyboard has been enabled and opened, as a usage statistic.

7. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features;
  • Process, verify, restore, and manage subscriptions and purchases;
  • Understand how features and subscription offers are used, measure performance, and decide what to build next;
  • Find and fix bugs, crashes, and usability problems;
  • Ask for your feedback or an App Store / Google Play review;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security problems;
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region with similar laws, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App and deliver the subscription or lifetime purchase you bought;
  • Legitimate interests — to understand usage, improve the App, keep it secure, and prevent fraud, balanced against your rights;
  • Consent — where required by law, for example for certain analytics or device permissions. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing;
  • Legal obligation — for tax, accounting, and responding to lawful requests.

9. Service providers and third parties

We share information only with the following providers, who process it on our behalf or as independent controllers as described in their policies:

  • Apple (App Store) — distribution, payments, subscriptions, refunds. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Play, Firebase Analytics) — distribution, payments, subscriptions, refunds, and app analytics. See Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • PostHog — product analytics and masked session replay, hosted in the European Union. See PostHog Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management and receipt validation. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Messaging and SMS apps you choose (for example WhatsApp) — receive the number and message only when you start a chat. Direct Message is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or any other messaging provider.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, to protect our rights, users, or the public, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information, or you will be notified).

10. Tracking and advertising

Direct Message does not show third-party advertising. We do not use your data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, we do not request Apple’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and we do not share your data with data brokers. Analytics data is used only to operate and improve Direct Message.

11. Retention

  • On-device data stays until you delete it in the App, clear App data, or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics and session replay data is kept only as long as needed for analysis and then deleted or aggregated, according to the retention settings of Firebase and PostHog (generally no longer than 14 months for Firebase event data; session replays are kept for a shorter period).
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as you have an active subscription or lifetime purchase, and afterwards for as long as required for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) to our providers and limiting access to analytics and purchase data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please also protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software up to date.

13. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Delete on-device data — use the delete options in the App, clear App data in your device settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Reset analytics identifiers — uninstalling the App deletes the installation identifier; a new, unrelated identifier is created if you reinstall.
  • Request deletion of analytics or purchase data — email us at info@washey.com. Because we do not have accounts, we may ask for information to locate your data (for example your store order or transaction ID shown in your Apple or Google purchase receipt). Deleting purchase records does not cancel a subscription — see the Terms for how to cancel.
  • Permissions — you can turn contacts access or the keyboard on or off at any time in your device settings.

We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

14. US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion or correction; and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. The categories we collect are listed in section 3 (identifiers, internet or other electronic activity, commercial information about purchases, and approximate location). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not process sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. To make a request, email info@washey.com.

15. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

16. Children

Direct Message is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases by minors should be made with the involvement of a parent or guardian, who can use Apple’s “Ask to Buy” or Google Play’s purchase approval and parental controls. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

17. App Store and Google Play disclosures

The App Store “App Privacy” label and the Google Play “Data safety” section for Direct Message summarise the practices described in this policy. If anything there appears to differ from this policy, this policy describes our practices in more detail; please contact us so we can correct it.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add features or change providers. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If the changes are significant, we will also notify you in the App. Continuing to use the App after an update means the updated policy applies.

19. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@washey.com. Please include “Privacy” in the subject line.
